Title: LyreTune - Ancient Greek Lyre Tuner

Subtitle (30 chars max): Tune your lyre accurately

Description:
------------
LyreTune helps you tune ancient Greek lyres and similar stringed instruments using your device's microphone.

The app detects musical pitches in real-time and shows you exactly which note you're playing, making it easy to achieve accurate tuning for historical music performance.

Features:
• Real-time pitch detection using your device's microphone
• Support for multiple ancient Greek musical scales (Lydian, Phrygian, Dorian, and more)
• Choice of temperaments including Pythagorean and Just Intonation
• Visual frequency display showing exact Hz and cents offset
• Adjustable A4 reference pitch (default 440 Hz)
• Works with lyres, kitharas, and other stringed instruments
• No internet connection required
• No ads or in-app purchases
• Privacy-focused: no data collection

Whether you're a historical music enthusiast, student, or professional performer, LyreTune provides the specialized tuning support you need for ancient Greek instruments.

The app processes audio entirely on your device. No recordings are stored or transmitted.
